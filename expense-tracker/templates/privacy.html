Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account and log your expenses. This includes your email address, account credentials, and the financial transaction details you choose to record (amount, category, date, and description).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. We use your transaction history to generate spending reports and summaries. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. Your data is stored securely, and we implement strict access controls to ensure that only authorized systems can interact with your information.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal financial information to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.app.
